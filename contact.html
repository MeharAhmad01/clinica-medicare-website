How can I book an appointment at Clinica Medicare?

02

What should I bring to my first consultation?

If available, bring your previous medical reports, relevant prescriptions, and details of any medicines you are currently taking. These may help the doctor understand your medical history.

03

How long does a homeopathic consultation take?

Consultation time may vary depending on your concerns, medical history, and the information needed by the doctor. Please contact the clinic for details about appointment scheduling.

04

Are treatments customized for each patient?

The doctor can assess your concerns and medical history to discuss a care plan based on your individual needs. Treatment options and expected outcomes may vary from person to person.
