Website: https://clinicamedicare.in/

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled in connection with your use of the Clinica Medicare website. Please read it carefully to understand our approach to privacy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, including:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Information included in appointment requests or inquiries.
  • Messages or other details you voluntarily submit to us.
  • Technical information associated with website usage, where applicable.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through general website forms or email. Information required for healthcare services may be handled separately in accordance with applicable requirements and the relevant service arrangements.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to us may be used, where applicable, for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your questions and inquiries.
  • Managing appointment requests and related communications.
  • Providing information about our healthcare services.
  • Communicating relevant appointment or service updates.
  • Maintaining and improving our website and user experience.
  • Addressing security issues and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Healthcare and Personal Information

If you contact Clinica Medicare regarding healthcare services, you should provide only information relevant to your inquiry. Information relating to your health should be shared through appropriate communication channels.

This website's general contact channels should not be assumed to be an emergency service or a secure medical records portal. Please do not use them to communicate information that requires immediate medical attention.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. Depending on the technologies used on this website, cookies or similar technologies may support website functionality, remember preferences, or help understand website usage.

You may be able to manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

The actual cookies used by this website depend on its current configuration and the services enabled.

5. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services, such as social media integrations, maps, fonts, or website analytics, depending on the features enabled.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Clinica Medicare does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective policies before sharing information with them.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not describe or promise any data-sharing practice beyond the arrangements actually used by the clinic. Where applicable, information may need to be handled by service providers supporting website operation or communications, or disclosed where required by law.

Information should be shared only for appropriate purposes and subject to applicable legal requirements and relevant safeguards.

7. Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting personal information. Appropriate safeguards should be applied to information handled through our website and related processes.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please take care when sharing personal information online.

8. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, operational, and record-retention requirements.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances in which it was provided.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to ask about personal information associated with you or to request correction or deletion where appropriate. You may also raise concerns about how your information is handled.

Requests may require verification of your identity and may be subject to legal or other applicable limitations.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to encourage children to disclose personal information unnecessarily. Where information concerning a child is provided in connection with healthcare services, it should be handled by the appropriate parent, guardian, or authorized person, subject to applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services, or applicable requirements change. Any revised version should be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
