Cookies Policy – Clinica Medicare

This Cookies Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit the Clinica Medicare website.

  1. Home
  2. Cookies Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They can help a website remember certain information about your visit and support website functionality and user experience.

2. How Cookies May Be Used

Depending on the features enabled on this website, cookies or similar technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality and navigation.
  • Remembering certain preferences where that functionality is available.
  • Understanding website performance and usage, if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Supporting embedded third-party features, where present.

3. Types of Cookies

Websites may use different types of cookies depending on the services and features they provide:

  • Essential cookies: These may be required for core website functions to operate properly.
  • Preference cookies: These may remember choices or settings you make on a website.
  • Analytics cookies: If analytics services are enabled, these may help website operators understand how visitors use the website.
  • Third-party cookies: Some external services embedded or linked on a website may use their own cookies under their respective policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Certain pages may contain links to social media platforms or other external websites. If third-party content or services are integrated into this website, those providers may collect information or use cookies according to their own policies. Please review the relevant third-party privacy and cookie policies for further information.

5. Managing Cookies

You can review the cookie and privacy settings available in your web browser. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that restricting certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

6. Personal Information

Cookies and similar technologies may sometimes be associated with information about your website visit. For information about how Clinica Medicare handles personal information, please contact us or review our Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated when website features, practices, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version should be published on this page with the revised information.
